# PRD: Spending summary card

**Run:** eval
**Date:** 2026-11-24

## Goal

Users want to see at a glance how much they have been spending lately, right on the dashboard.

## User stories

- As the user, I want a summary of my recent spending so that I notice when I overspend.
- As the user, I want big purchases to stand out so that I can review them.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The dashboard shows a card with the total of recent expenses.
2. The card loads quickly, even for users with a lot of expenses.
3. Large expenses are highlighted in the card.
4. The total is shown in the user's currency.

## Out of scope

- Budgets and spending limits.
- Exporting data.

## Assumptions

None.

## Open questions

None.
